# 🌍 AI-Powered Urban Air Quality Intelligence for Smart City Intervention

## 📖 Overview
AI-Powered Urban Air Quality Intelligence is a smart city solution that leverages Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) to monitor, analyze, and predict urban air quality. The platform integrates environmental and weather data to provide real-time insights, forecast Air Quality Index (AQI), and support data-driven decision-making. Through an interactive dashboard, the system helps government authorities, urban planners, and citizens take proactive measures to reduce pollution and improve public health.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement
Rapid urbanization, industrial growth, and increasing vehicular emissions have significantly impacted air quality in modern cities. Traditional monitoring systems provide only historical or current air quality data, making it difficult to predict pollution levels and take preventive action.

This project aims to develop an AI-powered solution that:
- Monitors real-time air quality.
- Predicts future AQI using Machine Learning.
- Identifies pollution hotspots.
- Supports smart city intervention through actionable insights.
